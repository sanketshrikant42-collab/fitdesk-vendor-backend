Payroll

Same rules as v1.2.0: daily rate × paid days, whole rupees, advances deducted, PT commission added.

How salary is worked out from attendance
Daily rate = monthly salary ÷ days in the month (“Calendar Days”) or ÷ 30 (“Fixed 30 Days”).
Paid days = Present + ½ for each Half Day + Paid Leave + Weekly Off + Holiday (if “Pay staff for Holiday days” is on in Settings) + every Sunday left unmarked.
Not paid: Absent, and any other day left unmarked. Days not marked are flagged below so they can be fixed before paying.
Salary = daily rate × paid days, rounded to whole rupees, minus advances and any deduction. PT commission for trainers is added on top.
Example: ₹30,000, 30-day month, 22 Present + 2 Half Day + 4 Sundays + 1 Holiday + 1 Absent → 28 paid days → ₹28,000.
